# owner-notifications

When blocked, produce a concise decision request with exact action, target, risk, budget, evidence and safe alternatives. Do not proceed until explicit owner approval is received. Notification delivery via MCP/Telegram/email is not certified until an authorized end-to-end test; never claim it was sent merely because it was drafted.
